Your CV preview will appear here.

04 — Job Application Dashboard

Keep track of applications, interviews, offers, and follow-up notes. Records are saved in this browser on this device.

Application overview

Export CSV creates a spreadsheet-compatible file that you can open in Excel or Google Sheets. This dashboard does not send applications or reminders automatically.

How this version works

  1. Save your master profile.
  2. Paste a job advertisement and prepare a tailored CV prompt.
  3. Use the prompt in ChatGPT and review the resulting CV.
  4. Preview and save the final CV as a PDF.
  5. Record and update your applications in the dashboard.

This free version does not call an AI model automatically. Verify dates, qualifications, skills, and achievements before applying.
