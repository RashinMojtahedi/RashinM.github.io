How this version works

  1. Maintain your master profile once.
  2. Paste a new job advertisement.
  3. Generate a role-specific prompt and send it to ChatGPT.
  4. Paste the reviewed CV into the final CV section.
  5. Preview and save the CV as a PDF.

This free version does not call an AI model automatically. Always verify dates, skills, qualifications, and achievements before applying.
